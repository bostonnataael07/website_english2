Simple Present Tense

Penjelasan
Digunakan untuk menyatakan kegiatan yang dilakukan secara rutin, kebiasaan, atau fakta umum.
Rumus
(+) S + V1 (s/es) + Object
(-) S + Do/Does + not + V1 + Object
(?) Do/Does + S + V1 + Object ?
Contoh Kalimat
  • (+)
    They play football every weekend.
  • (-)
    They do not play football every weekend.
  • (?)
    Do they play football every weekend?
